What's in the box

Everything you saw on screen, and every one of them real. Nothing here needs a second login.

MORNING

The register

Settled, unsettled, needed time or not in, beside each student. One quiet grey, so no student sees their name light up.

ALL DAY

Notes that save themselves

Type on a student's line and it is saved as you go. The kind of note is picked from your words.

CLEVER BIT

Linked to targets

Buddy suggests the step or EHCP outcome a note points to, and shows the words it matched. In our tests it put the right step first 38 times out of 40.

EVIDENCE

Slides, made for you

Add photos to a step and the slide is built, with a link ready to paste into BridgeLink.

NEW

EHCP outcomes in one paste

Copy a student's EHCP pillar from BridgeLink, paste it in, and every outcome arrives with first names swapped for initials.

HOME TIME

Wrap up

Safeguarding, follow ups, notes to link and what is already on BridgeLink. Then it says you are done.

REVIEWS

Review packs

A student's notes, steps and EHCP evidence in a Doc and a PDF. Confidential and safeguarding notes are always left out.

On the phone lines

Do I still need to use BridgeLink?

Yes. Buddy is where the day happens; BridgeLink is where the evidence goes. Buddy gets it ready, so the BridgeLink part takes minutes rather than an evening.

Can my TA use it too?

Add their school email in Setup and they open your base with the same link. They log notes and tick steps; Setup stays yours.

Where do the school's targets come from?

Jay adds each term's target sheets and dates, and they appear in everyone's Plan a term by themselves.

Is it really free?

Completely. Made in Base 5, for every class at Chilton Bridge.

How do I order?

Go to the Buddy page, press Start and sign in with your school Google account. About two minutes the first time.

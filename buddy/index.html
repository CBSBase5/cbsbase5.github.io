BridgeLink Buddy

by Jay

Daily notes, the register, targets and milestones, evidence slides and BridgeLink, all in one place for your base. Made in Base 5, free for every class at Chilton Bridge.

Start BridgeLink Buddy Sign in with your school Google account. About two minutes the first time.

What it does

✎Notes in seconds

Type in a student's card and it saves as you go. Positive, learning, conversation, off site: the kind of note lights up from your words.

★Targets and milestones

Plan a term from the school's target sheets in a few clicks. Tick steps as students reach them, with a milestone generator for the wording.

▦Evidence slides

Add photos to a milestone and Buddy makes the slide, with a presentation link ready to paste into BridgeLink.

✓Wrap up

At the end of the day it shows one next thing at a time: safeguarding, follow ups, BridgeLink. No long lists.

Your data stays yours

  • Starting makes a new, empty Google Sheet in your own Drive. Only you, and the helpers you add, can open it.
  • Jay cannot see your notes, and you cannot see his. Nothing goes on this website.
  • Students go in by initials only. Names never go in.
  • A copy of your Sheet is saved to your Drive every Friday afternoon, by itself.

Questions

How does my learning mentor or TA join?

Add their school email in Buddy under Setup, then Staff who can open this. Buddy shares your Sheet with them. They press Start on this page and choose your base from the list.

Helpers log notes and tick steps, but cannot change Setup.

Are confidential notes hidden from helpers?

In Buddy, yes: a note marked confidential only shows to you and the person who wrote it. Because helpers need your Sheet shared with them to save their notes, anything very sensitive belongs on the school's safeguarding system rather than in Buddy.

How do I get updates?

You do not need to do anything. When Jay improves Buddy, everyone has the new version the next time they open it. The version number is in the menu under your initials.

I help in two bases. Can I see both?

Yes. Once both teachers have added you, open the menu under your initials and choose Your bases to switch.

What if I stop using it?

Your Sheet stays in your Drive, in a folder called BridgeLink Buddy files, with your photos and backups. It is an ordinary Google Sheet, so you can keep it, download it or delete it.

Ideas or something not working?

Use Send feedback or an idea in the menu under your initials, or tell Jay.

Digital media

Every part of a computer has one job.

Taking things apart and working out what each bit was for. The baseline for this one is really just a conversation about what you already know.

InWhich parts do you put information in with?
OutWhich parts give information back out?
KeepWhich parts keep it when the power goes off?

That is the whole target for this half term, so this is the first go rather than the only one.

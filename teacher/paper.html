How the sheets work

Calm by design: each five minute chunk is one sheet on its own page, with the question, somewhere to answer and plenty of white space. Questions are redesigned for a pen (circle a letter, tick, write in a box, write A or B, number them, join with a line), pictures sit beside the words, and the screen toys have paper versions. Hints are kept off the question pages, on their own page after the activity, numbered to match. Optional bonus questions are left off unless you tick them in.

The first page says which level it is, with "fancy a different one? just say". The last page has a card to cut out and a "how was that?" line, so you hear if the level is off. Bonnie is hiding small on one sheet. Print double sided if you can; "Save as PDF" in the print box keeps a copy.

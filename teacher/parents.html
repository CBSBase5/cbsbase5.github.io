Before you change anything

The portal is public: anyone with the link can read it. Keep everything general. No student names, initials, photos or anything about one child. No trip places or times. Safeguarding contacts exactly as the school publishes them, and no personal numbers or addresses.

The timetable and "This week" come from the term file, the same one the students' pages use, so they are not changed here.

Lunch

The portal links the school lunch menu as a PDF. To add or replace it, save the menu as a PDF called lunch-menu.pdf, open the parents folder on GitHub, drop it in and commit. The link below is then /parents/lunch-menu.pdf. Or paste a link to a menu the school publishes. Base 5's lunch times and trip days come from the term file.

Putting it live

  1. Preview to see the portal with your changes (only on this device).
  2. Download parents.js.
  3. Open the data folder on GitHub, drop the file in and commit. It replaces the old one, and the portal updates within about ten minutes.

HQ DROPS

Staff only

Each HQ opens with a few rooms, and the rest open a little at a time through the term.

How it works

Every HQ now opens on a front door: the greeting, Start, their game, a line for anything new, and one button to everything else. Behind that button, Core practice, the check in, Choose a subject and Study with Bonnie are there from the first day. Everything else opens one school day at a time from Wednesday 4 November (never on a Tuesday), and the student sees "New in your HQ" on their front door the day it opens.

Tap a square to change it for one student: on the schedule, then open now, then held back, then back to the schedule. Their HQ picks it up the next time it loads on any device. Open all is for a student who copes well with everything at once.

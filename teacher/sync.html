SHARED MEMORY

Staff only

About ten minutes, once. After this, progress follows each student between devices, sends land in a sheet, folders come from the sheet, and the live view fills itself in.

1

Make a new Google Sheet

In your school Google Drive: New, then Google Sheets. Call it Base 5 shared memory. It stays in your Drive, private to you.

2

Open the script editor

In the sheet's menu: Extensions, then Apps Script. A new tab opens with a file called Code.gs.

4

Run setup, once

In the toolbar, pick setup in the function box, then press Run.

Drawn to look like the Apps Script editor.

Google asks for permission the first time. That is normal for any script, including your own:

  1. Review permissions, then pick your school account.
  2. If it says Google has not verified this app: press Advanced, then Go to Base 5 shared memory. It is your own script, so this is safe.
  3. Allow. It asks for the sheet (to read and write it), email (to tell you when something is sent, and for postcards home) and to run on a timer (the 3pm email).

Back in the sheet there are now these tabs. The main five:

FoldersOne row is one thing in a student's folder. Who, type (Message, Something to try, File), date, title, the text or link, a note. Tick Hide to take something out without deleting it. What is in the folders now is already copied in.
SendsEverything a student sends you, newest at the bottom, with a Seen column the live view fills in.
PeopleEach student's school email and their four number code, and each staff member's school email. Anyone signed in and listed here gets into their own private pages without typing a code.
SettingsThe staff passphrase (the backup way in for staff), whether each send is also emailed to you, the 3pm daily email (TRUE or FALSE), the weekly fuel target and its reward.
ProgressThe site writes this one. Leave it alone.

And the ones the newer features use:

TargetsAutumn 2 targets: one row each, with Pillar, a short Id that never changes (like eng1), Title, Statement, then each student's own milestone wording in their column. My targets and the staff Targets page read it.
TicksWhere target ticks and notes land, from students and from you. The site writes this one.
IdeasEvery "What should we add?" idea. Pick a Status (Thinking about it, Building it, Done), put a Link in once it is built, or tick Hide.
Wall and QuestionsQuestion Wall posts and student-made questions. Nothing goes out until you tick Approved (or approve it in the live view).
EventsThe timeline: check-ins, chunks, finishes, new bests, sends. The site writes this one.
Shoutouts, Votes, Fuel, Scores, PostcardsThe site writes these. In Votes you can tick Hide on any reason you would rather not show.

People has a fourth column, Parent email, for the Friday postcard.

If the school's Google account will not let scripts run at all, IT can switch on Apps Script for your account.

5

Deploy it as a web app

Deploy (top right), then New deployment. Press the cog next to Select type and choose Web app. Then:

  • Execute as: Me, so the script uses your sheet.
  • Who has access: Anyone within the school (it may show the trust's name). Only people signed in to a school Google account can reach it. The site talks to it through a small hidden page that uses the student's own sign-in, so students need to be signed in to their school Google account in the browser they use.

Press Deploy, allow again if asked, and copy the Web app URL. It ends in /exec.

6

Check it, then send it over

Paste the web app URL here and press Check. If it works, send the URL to Claude to switch it on for every device. Until then you can try it on this device only.

7

Open the live view

The live view opens by itself for staff listed in the People tab, or with the staff passphrase. It shows each HQ's progress and everything sent to you. On your own laptop, tick This is a staff device there, so trying an HQ never changes a student's progress.

+

Later on

  • Putting something in a folder: add a row to the Folders tab. It shows the next time the student opens their folder. No downloading or uploading.
  • Changing the script (if Claude sends a new version): paste it over the old one, Save, then Deploy, Manage deployments, the pencil, Version: New version, Deploy. The URL stays the same.
  • If a device cannot reach the sheet (the staff room says so at the top): check that browser is signed in to a school Google account. Some managed devices block signed-in Google pages inside other sites; on those, the site simply carries on saving on the device.
  • If the sheet is ever unreachable, nothing breaks: progress still saves on the device and catches up later, folders fall back to the files on the site, and sends offer Gmail as before.
  • Pictures (doodle pad and the like) still go by email, because a sheet cannot hold them well.

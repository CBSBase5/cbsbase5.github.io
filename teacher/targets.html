Where the targets come from

Easiest: put the link to your BridgeLink Buddy Google Sheet next to "BridgeLink Buddy sheet" in the Settings tab of the Base 5 sheet (run setup once to add that row). Then My targets and this page read each student's steps for the "Targets term" straight from Buddy, and show Buddy's status too. Change a step in Buddy and it changes here. Nothing to copy.

Or, without Buddy: pick the targets file (a .tsv, one row per step, from BridgeLink Buddy or from Claude) and this replaces every row of the Targets tab with it. A copy of the old rows is kept in a new tab first, so nothing is lost. The wording never goes into the site: it goes straight from your file to your sheet. Needs the October 2026 version of the script or later.

AUTUMN 2 DROPS

Staff only

An activity for every milestone, landing in each student's HQ folder a little at a time.

The term at a glance

Each activity teaches one skill and is shared by everyone whose milestone it covers, with three levels the student picks. The folder link suggests a starting level for each student. Nothing new lands on Tuesdays (trip day) and the last week is left free for catching up.

Put the plan in your Folders tab (optional)

You do not need to do anything for the drops to arrive: until the plan is in the sheet, each folder reads it from the site and shows each activity from its day. Putting it in the Folders tab gives you control: tick Hide to hold one back, or change its Date to move it. Once any Autumn 2 row is in the sheet for a student, the sheet decides for that student.

This needs the new version of the script (in the repo at teacher/sync/base5-sheet.gs.txt): paste it in, Save, then Deploy, Manage deployments, the pencil, Version: New version, Deploy. Rows already in the tab are never added twice, so it is safe to press again.

Day by day

Tap a student to see just theirs. Once the page has your passphrase, each drop shows started (chunks done) or done. Faded ones are still being written. Preview the library with everything in it.

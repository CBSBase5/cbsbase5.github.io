STUDENT FOLDERS

Staff only

The backup way to fill folders. With the shared memory on, folders come from the Folders tab of your Base 5 sheet: add a row there instead. This page only makes the files used when the sheet cannot be reached.

1. Whose folder?

Pick a student to open their folder.

Folders open without a code, and the site is public, so anyone with the link could read one. Messages and links are fine. Keep anything personal in Google Drive, shared with the student's school account only, and link to it.

2. Messages

Newest shows first. Blank lines make new paragraphs and web links turn into links. Your name goes on the bottom by itself. They see a New tag on anything they have not seen yet.

3. Things to try

Any page on the site, or anywhere else. They can tick "had a go" on their own device; that stays with them and does not come back here.

4. Files

Files are links, so the file itself stays wherever it lives. For anything personal, keep it in Google Drive, share it with the student's school account only, and paste the link here.

5. Save it

  3. About a minute later it is in their folder: see their folder.

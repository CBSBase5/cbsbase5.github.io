The village where the dinosaurs stayed

A model village, built small, where dinosaurs and people share the same streets. No words in the finished thing: the scenes tell it. This page is just for planning, and none of it is fixed.

Block 2

How big is small?

Model villages are built to a scale. The most common one, and the one with the most kits available, is OO scale. That means everything is 76 times smaller than the real thing, written as 1:76.

So a real person about 1.8 metres tall comes out at roughly 24mm. About the height of your thumbnail.

    Block 5

    Telling a story with no words

    This is the heart of your project. A model can only show one frozen moment, so every moment has to be doing work. A few things that make a still scene read as a story:

    • Where everyone is looking. If six figures all face the same way, the viewer looks there too.
    • The moment just after. A knocked over bin, a dropped shopping bag, a fence with a hole in it. Aftermath often tells more than action.
    • Distance. Who is close to the dinosaur and who is keeping well back tells you how dangerous it is, without a single word.
    • One thing that is wrong. Everything normal except one detail is unsettling in a good way.
    • Body position. Running, pointing, hiding, ignoring. Figures can often be gently repositioned with warm water.

    Build your scenes below. Add as many as you like. Three good ones beats ten thin ones, but that is your call.

Somewhere to find an idea for your project. Try any of these, in any order, or none of them. Nothing you pick here is final, and half an idea counts.

      It depends

        Anything in the “it depends” pile goes on your idea shelf by itself. Those are the ones people argue about, which makes them good projects.

        Mash it up

        Pick anything you are into. One is enough, more gives it more to play with.

        What could it be?

        Your project can be pretty much anything you can make or do. Tap any that you would not mind trying. You can pick more than one.

        What if...?

        Tap a start, then finish it in the box. Or ignore the starts and write your own.

        Ask someone

        Pick a question and ask it out loud to Jay, HB, Willow, or someone at home. Jot down anything they say that is interesting, or do not and just remember it.

        Your idea shelf

        Everything you have kept. Star the ones that grab you, bin the rest.

        Shape one up

        When one idea feels like it might be the one, give it a little shape. It does not have to be the final idea, and you can do this as many times as you like.

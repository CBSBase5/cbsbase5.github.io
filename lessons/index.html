M menu, D draw, T pause the timer, R random picker, P calm pause (a clicker's blank button works too), N prompt line, S snap the board for evidence, F full screen, E edit (staff).

Preview opens a lesson without it counting: nothing is marked taught, nothing goes to Buddy, and the timetable still opens it next. You can switch any lesson into preview, or reset it back to the start, from its menu (M), and the last slide has a Reset button too.

What has been taught is kept on the device you teach from, and topped up from the sheet: any lesson sent to Buddy counts as taught on every board, so a wiped browser or a different laptop picks up where you left off. Anything taught but not sent shows as Not sent here and in the staff room. The last slide marks a lesson taught. Tick who was here, then Send to Buddy: everyone there gets an Evidence row and a BridgeLink Buddy Learning note linked to the milestone step the lesson was built for, with the board picture (saved to Drive) as a photo. Anyone who missed it gets the matching HQ activity brought forward into their folder, if it hasn't landed already.

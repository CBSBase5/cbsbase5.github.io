Horticulture with Kara

Outside, in the beds.

Kara runs this one. Outside for three quarters of an hour, gloves on if you want them.

Garden Detective goes with it: build a plant and a tree part by part, find out what each part does, then zoom a microscope into soil, compost and bread to find the microbes doing the hidden work. Bring a leaf or a seed back in if you want something real to look at.

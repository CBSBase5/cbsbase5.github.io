Two truths and a lie

Three things about you. One is rubbish.

Two true, one made up. Everyone else works out which is which.

Like this

OneI have broken the same arm twice.
TwoI got stuck in a lift with a man dressed as a wizard.
ThreeI can name every county in England.

The specific ones are hardest to guess. "I have been to Spain" gives nobody anything to work with.

A user manual for you

How you work, in your own words.

Everything else comes with instructions. People do not, which is why so much of this goes wrong.

Pick one question. One line is a finished user manual.

01How do you like people to get your attention?
02What does it look like when you have had enough?
03What helps at that point?
04What makes it worse?
05What should someone know before asking you to do something?
06What could you talk about for an hour?
07What do people usually get wrong about you?

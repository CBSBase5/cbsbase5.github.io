In catch up

Two truths and a lie

Three things about you. One is rubbish.

Two true, one made up. Everyone else works out which is which.

Like this

OneI have broken the same arm twice.
TwoI got stuck in a lift with a man dressed as a wizard.
ThreeI can name every county in England.

The specific ones are hardest to guess. "I have been to Spain" gives nobody anything to work with.

A user manual for you

How you work, in your own words.

Everything else comes with instructions. People do not, which is why so much of this goes wrong.

Pick one question. One line is a finished user manual.

01How do you like people to get your attention?
02What does it look like when you have had enough?
03What helps at that point?
04What makes it worse?
05What should someone know before asking you to do something?
06What could you talk about for an hour?
07What do people usually get wrong about you?

The Question Wall

Ask anything. No name on it.
Step oneWrite a question on the big sheet.
Step twoAnyone can write an answer under it. Jay included.
Step threeIt stays up all week, so no rush.

Serious, daft, or genuinely useful. Past ones have gone from what happens if you swallow a magnet to whether Jay has ever been in trouble with the police.

If you would rather not write on it

Hand Jay a scrap of paper Only answer other people's

No writing up. If you would rather just be outside and not look for anything, that also counts.

In looking ahead

What this room is like

Not rules. Rules get written by people who are not in the room.

Five things to argue about. Pick whichever you have an opinion on.

01What makes a bad day in here worse?
02What should happen when someone needs to be left alone?
03How do we want disagreements to go?
04What is fair to expect of each other?
05What should never happen in here?

Whatever gets agreed goes on the wall in your words, not tidied into school language. If it turns out to be wrong in three weeks, it gets changed.

Pick the word of the week

Someone has to choose it. May as well be you.

Every week has a word. Where it came from, what it used to mean, and the odd places it turns up. Last year one of them had a whole tower defence game built round it.

The good ones sound like they should mean something else, or have a story behind them.

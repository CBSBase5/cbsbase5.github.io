Yesterday was about who is here. Today is about what we do with the time. Three things, none of which involve writing much.

What this room is like

Not rules. Rules get written by people who are not in the room. This is the other thing: what Base 5 is actually like to be in, decided by the people who have to be in it.

A few questions to argue about:

  • What makes a bad day in here worse?
  • What should happen when someone needs to be left alone?
  • How do we want disagreements to go?
  • What is fair to expect of each other, and what is not?
  • What is one thing that should never happen in here?

Whatever is agreed goes up on the wall in your words, not tidied up into school language. If it turns out to be wrong in three weeks, it gets changed.

Other ways to do this

  • Write your answers instead of saying them.
  • Listen and only weigh in if something sounds wrong to you.
  • Say nothing today and add something later in the week.

What goes in the term

If you go to the Base 5 home page and click through Weeks 2 to 7, they are all empty. That is not an accident.

Some of it will get filled in by Jay because it has to be. The rest is genuinely open. Things worth suggesting:

  • Something you want to know more about, however niche.
  • Somewhere worth going, near enough to get to and back.
  • A thing you would like to make, build, cook, or break.
  • A tool for the site that does not exist yet.
  • Something from last year worth doing again.

Suggestions do not have to be sensible, and nobody has to justify one. Not everything will happen, but nothing happens if nobody says it.

Pick the word of the week

Every week has a word. Where it came from, what it used to mean, and the strange places it turns up. Last year the word had an entire tower defence game built around it.

Nobody has picked one for next week. Put a word forward, or a few, and we will vote. Good ones are usually words that sound like they should mean something else, or ones with a story behind them.

How to put one forward

  • Say it out loud and it goes on the list.
  • Write it on the Question Wall and it goes on the list.
  • Tell Jay quietly and it goes on the list with no name attached.

In construction

What things do

Before making anything, finding out what the materials will put up with.

This term the aesthetic and creative work is sculpture. Today is not sculpture. Today is breaking things on purpose to find out how they behave.

TryWhat bends and then goes back?
TryWhat bends and stays bent?
TryWhat snaps with no warning?
TryWhat sticks to what, and what refuses?
TryWhat holds weight standing up?
ThenBuild the tallest thing that stays up on its own.

Other ways to do it

Work with someone Just test, do not build Draw what you would build instead

In english

Where does it say that?

One short text. The answers are all already in it.

Finding evidence means pointing at the line that proves it, rather than saying what you reckon. That is the whole skill.

01Read it, or have it read to you.
02Someone asks a question about it.
03You point at the bit that answers it.

Pointing counts. Reading it out counts. Writing it down is optional.

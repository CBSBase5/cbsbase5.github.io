The Base 5 Personnel File

Official documentation. Every base needs files on its people. This is yours, and you are in charge of what goes in it.
Form B5-ME · issued by the Department of Finding Out Who Is Here

Read this bit, or don't

The rules, such as they are: every single question is optional. Blank answers just don't print. You can be completely honest, completely ridiculous, or a suspicious blend of both, and nobody will know which is which. If a question is boring, the dice button will answer it for you. If typing is a lot, tap things instead. If the whole form is a lot, there is a button at the bottom that fills everything in with nonsense and you can just correct the bits it gets wrong about you.

Nothing leaves this device unless you decide to save or send your file at the end.

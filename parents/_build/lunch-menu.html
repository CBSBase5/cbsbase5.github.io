Chilton Bridge School

School lunch menu

Three weeks that take turns: Spark, Fuel and Glow. Every day there is a main, a vegetarian choice, a grab and go, and a pudding.

Which week is it?

    Also every day

    • Jacket potato, plain or with cheese, tuna mayo or beans
    • Chicken goujon wrap
    • Cheese panini, plain or with ham, pepperoni, tuna or tomato
    • Salad pot
    • Sandwich, roll, baguette or wrap, on white or brown
    • A yogurt or fruit pot instead of pudding
    • Gluten free bread, wraps and pizza

    Chilton Bridge is a nut free school.

    For allergen information about any dish, please ask the kitchen team or your child's base.

    Dishes can occasionally change on the day, and some days are themed.

        How the website works

        • Their own space

          Each student has an HQ with English and maths in short sections at their level. It saves as they go.

        • The plan for the day

          The home page shows what is on now and next, and each day has its own page.

        • Games

          Plenty, many made for the group. In an HQ, a game is earned by having a go at some learning first.

        Everything is offered rather than demanded. Students choose the order, can skip a question or stop whenever they need to, and every HQ page has a Done for now button.

        This half term the group is exploring If we could time travel, should we? Each student answers it through a project they choose.

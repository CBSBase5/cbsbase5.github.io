Trip

Out from nine until four.

This week it is Go Ape at Moors Valley: rope bridges, ladders and zip wires up in the trees, in a big country park and forest. The whole day, so lunch happens while you are out rather than back here. Jay will go through the plan before you leave.

UpGo up in the trees in a harness, if you fancy it.
DownStay on the ground and watch, walk the forest, or play the trails.
Not goingThat is fine too. Say so to Jay and there is a plan for you.

Wear clothes you can move in and closed toe shoes, and bring a coat. It is a forest in October.

TakeEar defenders, a hood, something in your pocket to hold.
OutYou can step away from whatever is happening at any point.
NearJay and the staff are with you the whole time.

If it gets much

Say so to any adult Go somewhere quieter with someone Wait in the minibus Ask to head back early

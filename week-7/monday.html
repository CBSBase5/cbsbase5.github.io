Aesthetic and Creative

Sculpture, at the end of the day.

The last proper slot of the day, and it is a making one rather than a writing one. This half term it is small sculptures: clay, card, wire, foil, papier mâché and whatever turns up.

TrySquash, bend and join a few materials and see what they will do.
MakeStart a small piece, or carry on with the one you had going.
Look backSay what works and what you would change.

New this week: Gallery Night. The gallery after closing, and you have the keys. Borrow tricks from real sculptors, build a maquette that has to balance, carve holes for the light, judge three odd pieces, then hang your own with a proper gallery label.

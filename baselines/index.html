BASELINES

Week 3

A starting mark, so that what comes next is the right level.

What these are for. Jay needs to know what you can already do. That is the whole reason for them. No grade, no mark out of ten, and nothing goes on a report.

Skip anything you do not fancy. A skipped question tells him something useful too, and stopping halfway is a reasonable place to stop. Everything saves as you go, so one of these can take a week if it wants to.

Maths

Three papers. Jay will say which one is yours, and it is fine to do only that one.
Foundation 1

Maths one

Counting, tens and ones, part-whole models, and naming shapes. Seventeen questions, 29 marks.

 Foundation 2

Maths two

Place value to 1,000, comparing numbers, adding and subtracting, scales and capacity. Seventeen questions, 31 marks.

 Foundation 3

Maths three

Big numbers, rounding, the four operations, and angles. Seventeen questions, 38 marks.

English reading

Three short texts each time, with the text kept on the screen next to the question.
Foundation 1

Reading one

A cafe poster, a job advert and an email. Fourteen questions, 16 marks.

 Foundation 2

Reading two

Two of the texts are about this school, including the picture plan. Sixteen questions, 20 marks.

 Foundation 3

Reading three

Walking, shopping online, and a medical centre. Longer texts. Seventeen questions, 24 marks.

English writing

Spelling and punctuation the page can mark, and two longer pieces that Jay reads.
Foundation 2

Writing two

Alphabetical order, plurals, punctuation and ten spellings, then a form to fill in and an email to a friend. Six questions, 35 marks.

 Foundation 3

Writing three

Alphabetical order, tricky plurals and ten spellings, then an email about your town and a review of a game or a film. Five questions, 36 marks.

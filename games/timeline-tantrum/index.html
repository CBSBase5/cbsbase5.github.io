How to play

Squads take turns. On your go, move one Snag, aim, then fire once. After firing you get 3 seconds to scarper. Knock the other squad into the water or down to 0 to send them home through a time portal.

Keyboard
← → walk, ↑ ↓ aim, hold Space to power up and let go to fire.
Enter jump, Backspace backflip, Tab kit, number keys to pick quickly, Esc pause.
Touch or mouse
Drag on the battlefield to aim. Hold Fire to power up, let go to fire. Tap the little map to look around.
Numbers worth watching
Angle is how far above flat you are aiming. Power is how hard you throw. Wind pushes Sling Stones, Fire Pots, Spears and Plasma Bolts, but not the bouncy ones. More chevrons means a stronger wind.
Each squad's own kit
Open the kit to see your era's special weapons. Some have limited ammo, and the superweapon unlocks at turn 10. The Vine Swing, Sand Sled and Jetpack are free moves: they do not use up your go.
Buried treasure
Watch for little glints in the ground. Blast near one to dig up a real artefact, then walk over it to add it to your Time Museum. Some finds should not be there at all.
Crates
Bonnie sometimes pops out of a time portal with a crate. Walk over it for a snack, or, very rarely, something special.
Need a pause?
Breather passes your turn with no penalty. Pause any time; the match saves itself.

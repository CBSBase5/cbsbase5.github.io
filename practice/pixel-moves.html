Before you start

Move it, flip it, turn it, grow it

Bonnie and a few other pixel shapes need moving around the Memory House. Every chapter starts with a quick explainer, then you move shapes yourself: by typing a vector, or by tapping the squares where the shape ends up. You can drag across squares to paint faster.

Every puzzle can be tried again as many times as you like, and Show me is always there. Pick a chapter, or start at the beginning.

Writing Practice: Paper 6

Two writing tasks from a real Level 2 paper. Take them one at a time, together, or just have a look around - it all counts.
Task 1: an email, 21 marks Task 2: an online form, 15 marks Real exam time: 60 minutes

Who's writing today?

Pick your initials if you'd like your work saved with your name on it. Skipping is fine too.

What gets the marks

The examiner is looking at two separate things. You get marks for both, so a few small habits go a long way.

What you say and how you shape it

  • Answer every bullet point in the task
  • Use paragraphs - one idea per paragraph
  • Link ideas with words like Firstly, However, In addition
  • Match the tone: friendly but polite for the email, a bit more formal for the form

Spelling, punctuation and grammar

  • Capital letters and full stops everywhere they belong
  • Commas and apostrophes where needed
  • Keep tenses steady - do not drift between past and present
  • Read your work back at the end and fix what you spot

A quick plan before you write - even three scribbled words per bullet point - is the single biggest mark-winner. There's a planning box under each task if you want one.

Task 1 - 21 marks

An email about Healthy Living Week

Here's the email you're replying to:

From: lukas@communitycentre.web
To: you@yourmail.biz
Subject: Healthy Living Week

Hi everyone,

Healthy Living Week is coming to our Community Centre soon. We have lots of health and fitness sessions planned, including:

  • healthy eating workshops
  • street dance sessions
  • martial arts training
  • football and netball competitions.

Please let us know which sessions you would like to take part in.

Also tell us which other activities should be included and why. We'd love your ideas.

Lukas
Community Centre Manager

Your task

Write an email back to Lukas giving your ideas about Healthy Living Week. In your email, you should:

  • explain which sessions you would like to take part in and why
  • describe the other activities that you think should be included
  • say why these activities would be popular.

Aim for about 250 to 300 words. In the real exam you'd have about 35 minutes for this one.

Task 2 - 15 marks

An online form: the canteen proposal

Feedback form - www.yourorganisation.web/feedback

Your workplace/college/school is proposing to ban the sale of meat and fish in the canteen. In future only vegan and vegetarian food would be served.

You decide to give your views using the catering team's online form.

Your task

Write to the catering team to give your views on the proposal. In your form, you should:

  • say whether or not you agree with the proposal
  • give detailed reasons to support your views.

Aim for about 200 to 250 words. In the real exam you'd have about 25 minutes for this one. There's no right answer - agree, disagree, or sit on the fence, as long as you back it up.

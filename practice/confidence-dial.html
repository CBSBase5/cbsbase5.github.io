THE CONFIDENCE DIAL

Preparation for adulthood

Too little and you freeze. Too much and you crash. Find the sweet spot at home, at school and at work.

Confidence is a dial, not a switch. Turned right down, people freeze and miss out. Cranked right up, people rush in and things go wrong. Somewhere in the middle is the bit that works.

Seven stages: spot your strengths, play with the dial, try some confidence tools, log your past wins, make some decisions and build a plan card. Any order, skip anything, and it saves as you go.

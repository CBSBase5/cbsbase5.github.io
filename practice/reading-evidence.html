Retrieval and evidence

Reading, 30 marks, four short parts. Two extracts, a cellar nobody has been in for years and a river that is not keen on visitors.
Before you start

The answers are already in the text. That is the whole game.

Retrieval means finding the bit that is already written down. Evidence means copying that bit exactly, then saying what it proves. No guessing, no vibes, no staring at the page hoping it gives in first.

The extract stays on screen while you answer, and you tap the quote you want rather than copying it out. Some parts mark themselves. The ones about explaining are yours to mark, with the mark scheme shown, because a computer cannot tell whether your reason actually makes sense.

The statement

"The author makes the river crossing seem impossible for the travellers."

How far do you agree? Give a clear answer, because "it feels a bit dodgy" is not quite analysis. Cover the river itself, then the state of the raft, and quote the text for both. Your opinion needs receipts.

THE BRAVE BIT

Preparation for adulthood

Every new thing has a brave bit. Here is how to get through it without freezing or face planting.

Confidence is not about never being nervous. It is about getting through the brave bit: the first word, the first step, the first go.

Seven stages: follow Robin through a week, fill a proof jar, run a shop, sort some decisions, rehearse a script, spot risky confidence and pick your own brave bit. Any order, skip anything, and it saves as you go.

Preparation for adulthood

Plan a party for a group. Feed everyone, keep every guest happy, and make the money last.

Party time

One party, one budget, a whole group to feed

Pick a party below. The number of guests and the money change with it. Then plan, compare, build the list and pay at Hilltop Market, a pretend shop with real 2026 style prices.

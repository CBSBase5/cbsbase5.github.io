A day at school

Everyday English. Build a story one bit at a time. It can be a real day, a made-up day, or a completely ridiculous day.
Before you start

You are the writer, so you are in charge

You will build a short story about a day at school, one paragraph at a time. Six parts, and each one has a little guide and some first-line ideas if you want them. Nothing is compulsory, blank parts just get left out, and you can jump around the parts in any order using the dots.

None of this is marked or scored. It is your writing. Real day, invented day, a day where the vending machine gains consciousness, all completely allowed. At the end you can read the whole thing back and, if you like, send it to Jay.

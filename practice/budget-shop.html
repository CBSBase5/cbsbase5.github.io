Welcome in

One shop, one budget, a week of food

Bridge Mart is a pretend supermarket with real 2026 style prices. Pick a mission, plan the meals, then shop for them without running out of money.

There are seven stages. Go in any order, skip anything, come back later. It saves on this device as you go.

LEVEL 2 ENGLISH: SIX STOPS

About 45 minutes

One lesson's worth of revision, a stop at a time. Each stop has a quick look at Pass Functional Skills and a go at it here.

How it works. Six stops along the line, plus a start. The minutes are a guide for fitting it into a lesson, not a race. Go in order or jump about, skip anything, come back later. It saves itself.

Every stop links to the matching page on Pass Functional Skills if you want the longer version. It opens in a new tab, so this page stays where it is.

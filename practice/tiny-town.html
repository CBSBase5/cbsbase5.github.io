Model workshop

Shrink real things to a scale. Get it wrong and the model comes out silly.

Learn a bitskip it if you know this already

1 : 100 means 1 cm on the model stands for 100 cm in real life. So divide by 100. For 1 : 50 divide by 50, and for 1 : 10 divide by 10.

Handy trick: dividing by 50 is the same as dividing by 100, then doubling.

Planning office

Lay out the town square on a plan drawn to scale. Then measure real distances off it.

Learn a bitskip it if you know this already

Count squares from the middle of one thing to the middle of the other, along a row or a column. Then times by 5 for metres.

Going the other way, from real metres onto the plan, divide by 5.

Bus depot

Plan the Tiny Town bus loop on the town map, then turn map centimetres into real distance.

Learn a bitskip it if you know this already

So the number of map centimetres, times 100, gives real metres. 1,000 m is 1 km.

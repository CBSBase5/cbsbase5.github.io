Supermarkets have thought about this more than you have

That is the whole trick. The layout, the offers, the stuff at eye level: all designed by people whose job is getting your money off you. This is about thinking slightly harder back, using nothing more complicated than adding up.

The one bit worth keeping

Base, protein, veg. Bought in bulk, cooked in double.

That is not a school rule. It is how most people who are good with money actually eat, and it takes about as long as waiting for a takeaway.

Chalky is chalking himself. Pick a level when you're ready.

The race

You and Chalky share every ball. Whoever pots the number shown first wins the level. Pot a ball and you go again.

Aiming

Tap or click the table to point the cue, then lock it. Nudge buttons or the arrow keys fine tune it. The dotted line shows where the white goes.

Power

Once the aim is locked the power bar swings. Tap the table, press Space or hit Shoot to stop it and play the shot.

Warm ups

A quick sequences question pops up before each of your turns. Right, wrong or "show me", you still take your shot. They get trickier as the levels climb.

Send it to Jay

Only if you want to. It sends how far you got and which kinds of warm up went well, nothing else.

GARDEN DETECTIVE

Science · plants and microbes

Plants from root to seed, and the tiny life hiding in the soil, the bread bin and on your hands.

Start here

Seven seed packets. Open any of them.

Half of this is plants: their parts, their jobs and how they grow. The other half is microorganisms, the living things too small to see, which are busy in every garden. Handy before or after Horticulture with Kara.

The packets go roughly from naming things to explaining things. Any order is fine, every packet can be skipped, and it all saves on this device as you go.

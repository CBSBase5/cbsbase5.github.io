Mobile Catering Pitches in Hurstbourne

There are a number of mobile catering pitches in Hurstbourne. The council look forward to applications from new and interesting food businesses to trade, as well as the more traditional ice cream and burger sellers that always attract high numbers of customers. All pitches cost £40, whether given to vans, carts or bikes, and all traders are required to book pitches at least 3 months before beginning to sell food. A further £30 will be needed for a trading permit, which can be obtained directly from the council at the town hall.

Seafront

There are four mobile catering pitches on the seafront, two of which are only available during the summer. Ice cream traders are particularly welcomed on the beach itself, where cold food and drink is in high demand.

Parks and gardens

Hurstbourne has a range of gardens with pitches for food traders, including permanent spaces at the water gardens next to the beach. Following so many requests for ice cream in our parks, the council has opened up two spaces dedicated to ice cream bikes in the rose gardens at Portdown Park. Pitch boundaries are marked out on the ground and traders must stay within the lines and cease trading at 4pm. Anyone trading after this time will be fined.

A further two pitches are available at Elm Gardens in the café area next to the herb gardens. Traders should note that this site is closed during winter.

At all parks and gardens, traders are responsible for clearing any rubbish from their pitch.

Hurstbourne market

This is held in Hurstbourne High Street every Tuesday throughout the year. There is space for up to five mobile caterers. Phone 06668 968 683 for more information.

Events

During the summer months, there are a number of one-off events in the town. Phone 0800 968 555 for more information.

Regulations

All mobile caterers must obtain a trading permit and hold a Level 2 Food Hygiene and Safety certificate before trading.

GALLERY NIGHT

Aesthetic and creative

The sculpture gallery has closed for the night. The lights are low. You have the keys.

Seven rooms, any order. Meet some famous sculptors and borrow their tricks, balance a maquette, punch holes for light, play with surfaces, judge some pieces, fix a first draft, then hang your own piece with a gallery label.

Have some clay, card, foil or wire nearby if you fancy making a little model for real as you go. Every room is open. Skip anything. It saves itself on this device.

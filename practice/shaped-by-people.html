SHAPED BY PEOPLE

Physical world

Roads, bridges, parks and reservoirs. How people have built, dug and changed the places around us.

Meet Bridgeford. A made up town in an English valley. Slide it through 250 years and watch people change it: a ford becomes a bridge, fields become houses, a railway becomes a cycle path.

Seven stages, any order. Skip anything that is not for you today. The last one takes you outside, to survey the real streets around school.

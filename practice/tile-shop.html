Stage 1 · Will it tile?

No gaps, no overlaps

Test shapes on a shop floor and find out which ones tessellate.

Learn a bit

A shape tessellates when copies of it cover a floor with no gaps and no overlaps, going on for ever.

Tilers love shapes that tessellate. Nobody wants a bathroom floor with holes in it.

Learn a bit

Where tiles meet, their corners go all the way round a point. A full turn is 360°.

So a shape tiles on its own when its corners can add up to exactly 360°.

Pick a shape, then add tiles round the purple point one at a time.

Pick one: a regular pentagon has corners of 108°. Why will it not tessellate on its own?

Want to explain it in your own words? Why do some shapes tessellate and others do not?

Stage 2 · Turn and flip

Tiles on the cutting board

Reflect and rotate tile offcuts on a grid, square by square.

Learn a bit

Reflect: flip over a mirror line. Every square ends up the same distance away on the other side.

Rotate: turn about a centre point. You need the centre, the angle (90° or 180°) and the direction.

The tracing paper button shows the move happening, like a real piece of tracing paper.

Stage 3 · Robot tiler

A robot that speaks vector

Read vectors, write vectors, and drive the tiling robot.

Learn a bit

A column vector is two numbers stacked in tall brackets.

Top number: across. Positive is right, negative is left.
Bottom number: up or down. Positive is up, negative is down.

So 43 means 4 right and 3 up. -2-5 means 2 left and 5 down.

Stage 4 · The till

Sale on! Everything in 5% steps

Build any discount from 10% and 5% blocks, then serve the customers.

Learn a bit

10%: divide by 10. 5%: half of that.

Every multiple of 5% is built from those two blocks. 35% = 10% + 10% + 10% + 5%.

The sale price is the old price take away the discount.

Stage 5 · Pattern detective

How was this floor made?

Spot the move that takes each tile to the next one.

Learn a bit

Every tile in the shop has a little bird on it, so you can see which way it faces.

Translation: slid, nothing else. Reflection: flipped like a mirror image. Rotation: turned round.

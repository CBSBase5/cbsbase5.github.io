GOAL COACH

PE · training goals

Pick a sport, find out what it asks of a body, test yourself, then build a goal that actually fits you.

How this works. Seven stages, in any order you fancy. Skip anything that is not for you. It saves itself on this device, so you can stop and come back.

The tests in here are not a fitness test. They are a starting line you only ever compare with yourself. If something hurts in a sharp way, stop. That is the whole safety talk.

    Reaction time

    Tap the panel to start. When it turns green, tap as fast as you can. Five goes. Smaller numbers are quicker.

    Fun fact: in sprinting, a start less than 0.1 seconds after the gun counts as a false start, because people are not thought to be able to react that fast.

    Tap speed

    Your first tap starts a 10 second clock. Tap the drum as many times as you can. Stop any time.

    Ready when you are

    Balance

    Stand near a wall or a chair. Lift one foot, press start. Press stop when the foot touches down. It stops itself at 60 seconds.

    Plank hold

    Forearms and toes, or forearms and knees. Both count. Stop as soon as your back starts to sag. Short and solid beats long and wobbly. It stops itself at 2 minutes.

    5

    Goldilocks goals

    Too easy, too hard, or just right?

    Good place to start
    Learn a bit

    The best goals sit in the stretch zone: hard enough that you have to practise, doable enough that you can see yourself getting there.

    Too easy: comfort zoneYou could do it today. No practice needed, so nothing changes.
    Just right: stretch zoneA bit wobbly at first. With practice, you get there. This is where you improve.
    Too hard: panic zoneSo far away it feels impossible, so most people give up. Sometimes not safe either.

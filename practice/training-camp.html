TRAINING CAMP

PE · training goals

Pick a challenge, work out what it asks of you, test yourself, then plan the weeks that get you ready.

How camp works. Seven stages, any order you fancy. Skip anything that is not for you. It saves on this device, so you can stop and come back.

Not going on the ropes trip? Totally fine. Pick any challenge, or make up your own. How hard you go is always your call, and if something hurts in a sharp way, stop.

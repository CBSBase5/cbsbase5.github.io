English, working towards a qualification. Retrieval, evidence and inference, from two brothers and a shoe box of letters.
Before you start

Two shoe boxes of letters

In Tiny Legends, Clement kept every letter his brother ever sent him. Here are the first two, from 1957 and 1958, and a short piece his granddaughter wrote after finding them.

The letters stay at the top of the screen while you answer, and the answers are always in them somewhere. Some questions ask you to tap the sentence that proves something, instead of copying it out. The last one asks for a proper written answer, which goes to Jay unmarked. Laid out like a Functional Skills reading paper, so it counts as practice for that too.

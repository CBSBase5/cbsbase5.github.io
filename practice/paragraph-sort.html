THE SORTING OFFICE

English · paragraphs

Sentences arrive in a jumble. Sort them, stamp them, chop them and link them, then pack your own.

What this is. A sorting office where sentences come in muddled, and paragraphs go out tidy. Six bays, any order, and every one can be skipped. It saves as you go.

Who is this?

Optional. Pick your initials to see where to start, or just have a look around.

The sorting office floor

Bay 1

The sorting belt

Sentences that belong together go in the same paragraph.

Learn a bit

One paragraph, one idea

A paragraph is a group of sentences about the same thing. Think of it as a parcel: everything packed inside belongs together.

When a sentence is about something different, it goes in a different parcel.

Try a bit

Parcels are coming down the belt. Drag each one into the bin it belongs in, or just tap a bin to send it there.

Not for you right now? That is fine.Skip to bay 2

Bay 2

The label stamp

Every paragraph starts with a label: the topic sentence.

Learn a bit

The label on the parcel

The first sentence of a paragraph is usually the topic sentence. Like a label on a parcel, it tells the reader what is inside.

The sentences after it add the details.

Try a bit

Each parcel has been opened and its sentences muddled. Find the label first, then put the details back in order.

Bay 4

Linking lane

Couple your paragraphs together so the writing flows.

Learn a bit

Couplings between parcels

A linking word or phrase at the start of a paragraph tells the reader how it connects to the one before.

Some add more (Also). Some change direction (However). Some move time on (Later that day). Some show a result (As a result).

Part A Tag sorting

Tap a luggage tag, then tap the crate for its job.

Part B Couple the review

A review of a trip to Roarsome Valley dinosaur park. Tap a coupling, then tap a linking phrase to fit it. Some couplings take more than one.

Skip it if it is not for you.Skip to bay 5

Bay 5

The planning shelf

Label the boxes before you pack them.

Learn a bit

Plan the boxes first

Before writing, decide what each paragraph will be about. Give each box a label, put them in a sensible order, and jot a few ideas in each.

Notes are fine here. Nobody writes full sentences on a plan.

Try a bit

Pick something to write about. You can change anything on the plan whenever you like.

Rather skip the plan? Bay 6 has ready made plans too.Skip to bay 6

Bay 6

Pack and post

Write your own paragraphs, one box at a time.

Each box is one paragraph. Start with a label sentence, keep to the box's idea, and link it to the box before. The stamps light up as you write. Nothing here is marked, it just gives you hints.

Learn a bit, try a bit

Punctuation marks are road signs for sentences: they tell the reader when to stop, slow down, swerve, or brace themselves. This one goes from full stops to inverted commas. 25 quick questions plus one bit of writing at the end, which is not marked by the computer. Nothing is timed and nothing is a test.

Put it on this tablet's home screen

Then it opens from its own lunch bowl icon, like an app.

  1. Make sure this page is open in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Tap the new Lunch orders icon, and sign in with your school Google account the first time.
  1. Make sure this page is open in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.
  4. Tap the new Lunch orders icon, and sign in with your school Google account the first time.

After this, opening this page on this device goes straight to lunch orders. To see these steps again, add ?setup to the end of the address.

Using it at the hatch or in the kitchen

To keep a shared tablet on lunch orders: on an iPad, turn on Guided Access (Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access, then triple click the side or home button with lunch orders open). On Android, use App pinning (Settings, Security, App pinning).
